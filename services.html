Every service, and what it actually includes

Home Shifting in Bangalore

Full-flat and single-room household moves. We dismantle beds and wardrobes, wrap furniture, pack room by room with labelled cartons, load, transport, unload and reassemble at the new address. Unpacking is optional and priced separately.

Office and Corporate Relocation

Workstations, cabins, server racks, filing and IT equipment, moved against a numbered inventory so nothing goes missing. Most office moves run overnight or over a weekend so your team is working again on Monday.

Car Transportation

Cars moved on an open carrier or in an enclosed body depending on the vehicle and the route. Quoted as a separate line, so you can add or drop it without changing the rest of the move.

Bike and Two-Wheeler Transport

Two-wheelers wrapped and secured, and crated where the route or the vehicle calls for it. Commonly booked alongside a household move or on its own.

Packing and Unpacking

We bring the material: double-wall cartons, bubble wrap, corrugated sheets, stretch film, foam and tape. Fragile items, glass and artwork are packed separately and marked.

Loading and Unloading

Crew-only service if you have arranged your own vehicle, or if you are moving within the same building or society. Priced by crew size and time.

Warehousing and Storage

Short and long-term storage for when your handover dates do not line up. Goods stay packed and inventoried, and you pay for the period you actually use.

Factory and Workshop Shifting

Machinery, tools, stock and industrial fit-outs, with crating and the right lifting equipment. Common around the Peenya and Jalahalli industrial belts.

Courier, Cargo and Part-Load

Part-household and commercial consignments moved as a shared load, which costs less than a dedicated vehicle when you are not filling one.

Luggage Door-to-Door Delivery

Boxes, trunks and personal luggage collected from your door in Bangalore and delivered to the destination address, for students, working professionals and hostel moves.

Goods Insurance in Transit

Transit cover is available as an optional line item. We explain what is and is not covered before you decide, rather than assuming it into the price.

How we price a move

We do not publish a rate card, because a rate card either overcharges the small jobs or undercharges the big ones. Instead every job is surveyed and quoted individually. These are the things that move the number:

  • Volume - how much there actually is, which decides the vehicle and the crew.
  • Access - floor, lift availability and whether a truck can reach the door at both ends.
  • Packing scope - full packing, partial packing, or transport only.
  • Distance - within the locality, across Bangalore, or intercity.
  • Add-ons - vehicle transport, storage, transit insurance, unpacking.

The survey and the quote are free, and the quote is itemised so you can drop anything you do not want.

Questions about our services

Do you handle both household and commercial moves?

Yes. Households, offices, factories and warehouses, plus vehicles and part-load consignments.

Can I book only loading and unloading?

Yes. If you have your own vehicle, or you are moving inside the same building, we will quote crew and time only.

Do you supply the cartons and packing material?

Yes, it is included in the packing scope. You do not need to source anything yourself.

Is transit insurance compulsory?

No. It is optional and quoted separately so you can see exactly what it costs and what it covers.

Do you dismantle and reassemble furniture?

Yes, beds, wardrobes, modular units and workstations, as part of the packing and unpacking scope.
