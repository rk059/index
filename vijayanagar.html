What changes the price of a move in Vijayanagar

We do not publish a fixed rate card, because two flats of the same size rarely cost the same to move. The factors that genuinely move the number are:

  • Volume, not floor area. The number and size of items decides the vehicle, the crew size and the packing material.
  • Floor and lift access. A third-floor flat with no service lift takes materially longer than a ground-floor unit.
  • Vehicle reach. If a truck cannot reach the door, a smaller shuttle vehicle is added and the job takes an extra leg.
  • Packing scope. Full packing by our crew costs more than transport-only, where you pack and we carry.
  • Distance and destination. Within Vijayanagar is cheapest; cross-city and intercity add fuel, tolls and driver time.
  • Extras. Vehicle transport, transit insurance, storage and unpacking are priced separately so you can drop what you do not need.
