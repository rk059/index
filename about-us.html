Whether you're relocating across the street or across the country, our team of skilled professionals is dedicated to ensuring that your belongings are handled with the utmost care and arrive safely at their destination.

How a move with us actually runs

  1. Survey. Free, at your address or over a video call. We list what is going, check the floor, the lift and whether a vehicle can reach the door.
  2. Written quote. Itemised: labour, material, vehicle, and every optional extra priced separately so you can drop what you do not need.
  3. Confirmation. Date, time window and crew confirmed. You get a contact number for the day.
  4. Packing. Our crew packs and labels room by room and prepares an inventory before anything is loaded.
  5. Loading and transit. Loaded, secured and dispatched. For intercity moves you get updates until the vehicle reaches the destination state.
  6. Delivery. Unloading, placement, and reassembly or unpacking if that was in the agreed scope.
