How to check a "near me" mover before you book

  1. Ask for a survey, not a phone quote. A price given without seeing your goods is a guess, and guesses get revised upward on moving day.
  2. Get the quote in writing. It should list labour, material, vehicle, and every extra separately.
  3. Confirm who actually turns up. Ask whether the crew is the company's own or subcontracted, and get a name and number for the day.
  4. Check the vehicle can reach your door. On narrow Bangalore lanes this decides whether you need a shuttle load, which affects both time and cost.
  5. Ask what insurance covers. Transit cover is usually optional and priced separately; make sure you know what is and is not included.
  6. Avoid large advance payments. A reasonable booking amount is normal; paying most of the cost up front is not.
