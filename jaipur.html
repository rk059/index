Bangalore to Jaipur Packers and Movers

Tirupati Packers and Movers move households, office set-ups and vehicles from Bangalore to Jaipur in Rajasthan. It is a long-haul route of roughly 2,000 km by road, so it is planned as usually a shared or part-load consignment unless you book a dedicated truck.

Over long distances a dedicated truck for one household is expensive, so most customers choose a part-load. Booking early matters more here, because departures are scheduled.

We also handle the return leg, Jaipur to Bangalore, and moves onward from Jaipur to other cities in Rajasthan. Call +91 98125-18027 with your inventory and destination pin code for a written quote.

How a Bangalore to Jaipur move runs

  1. Survey. A free pre-move survey at your Bangalore address, or over a video call, to list what is actually going.
  2. Written quote. An itemised price covering packing material, labour, the vehicle, tolls and any optional transit insurance.
  3. Packing day. Our crew packs, labels and prepares an inventory before anything is loaded.
  4. Loading and dispatch. Goods are loaded, the vehicle is sealed where applicable and the consignment is dispatched towards Jaipur.
  5. Transit updates. You get a contact number for the vehicle and updates until it reaches Rajasthan.
  6. Delivery and unpacking. Unloading at the Jaipur address, with unpacking and reassembly if that was included in the quote.

What decides the cost from Bangalore to Jaipur

  • Distance. At roughly 2,000 km this is a long-haul run, and fuel, tolls and driver time scale with it.
  • Dedicated vs shared vehicle. A dedicated truck leaves when you want and goes direct. A shared consignment is cheaper but departs when the load is made up.
  • Volume. A one-bedroom consignment and a four-bedroom consignment need very different vehicles.
  • Packing scope. Full professional packing, partial packing, or transport only.
  • Access at both ends. Floor, lift availability and whether a truck can reach the door in Bangalore and in Jaipur.
  • Add-ons. Transit insurance, vehicle transport, storage and unpacking are quoted separately.

We do not quote a per-kilometre rate without seeing the inventory, because it is the volume and the access, not just the distance, that decide the real cost.

Bangalore to Jaipur moving questions

How far is Jaipur from Bangalore by road?

About 2,000 km. We plan it as a long-haul intercity route, usually a shared or part-load consignment unless you book a dedicated truck.

Can you transport my car or bike to Jaipur as well?

Yes. Vehicles travel on a carrier or in an enclosed body and are quoted as a separate line on the same job, so you deal with one team for both.

Is my consignment insured in transit?

Transit insurance is available and is quoted as an optional line item. We will explain what it does and does not cover before you decide.

How far ahead should I book?

For a dedicated vehicle, a week is comfortable. For a shared consignment, more notice gives you a better chance of a departure date that suits you. Month-end is always the busiest period.

Do you also move from Jaipur back to Bangalore?

Yes, and onward from Jaipur to other cities in Rajasthan. Tell us both addresses and we will quote the direction you need.
