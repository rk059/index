Moving in Bangalore: common questions

Which parts of Bangalore do you serve?

The whole city. Our office is at Kadugodi in Whitefield and we run crews across north, east, west and south Bengaluru, including the airport belt around Yelahanka and Devanahalli.

How much does a house shift in Bangalore cost?

It depends on volume, floor and lift access, packing scope and distance, not on floor area alone. We survey first, free of charge, and then give you an itemised written quote.

Do you move on Sundays and public holidays?

Yes. We work Monday to Sunday, 8:00 am to 9:00 pm. Many Bangalore societies actually prefer weekend moves because the service lift is free.

Can you handle a same-day move inside Bangalore?

Often, if a crew and vehicle are free. Month-end and weekends are the busiest, so give us two to three days' notice where you can.

Do you provide packing material, or should I buy it?

We bring our own cartons, bubble wrap, corrugated sheets, stretch film and tape. It is part of the packing scope in the quote.
