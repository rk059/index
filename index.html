Frequently asked questions

How much do packers and movers cost in Bangalore?

There is no single rate. Cost is driven by volume, floor and lift access at both ends, how much packing you want us to do, and the distance. We survey free of charge and then give you an itemised written quote, so nothing is added on moving day.

Do you do the packing, or do I?

Either. Full packing with our own material is the usual choice. If you prefer to pack yourself we will quote transport, loading and unloading only, which costs less.

Can you move my car or two-wheeler as well?

Yes. Vehicles go on a carrier or in an enclosed body and are quoted as a separate line on the same job.

Do you offer storage?

Yes, short and long-term, for when your possession dates do not line up. It is priced by the period you actually use.

Which areas of Bangalore do you cover?

All of Bengaluru. We are based at Kadugodi in Whitefield and run crews across the city, including the airport belt around Yelahanka, Jakkur and Devanahalli.

How far in advance should I book?

Two to three days for a local move, and about a week for an intercity move. Month-end and weekends are the busiest.
