Bangalore to Ranchi Packers and Movers

Tirupati Packers and Movers move households, office set-ups and vehicles from Bangalore to Ranchi in Jharkhand. Goods on this route travel along NH44 to Vijayawada, then NH16 and NH20 inland, passing Vijayawada, Visakhapatnam and Sambalpur, over roughly 1,750 km of road.

At about 1,750 km this is a genuinely long run. Expect roughly 4 to 6 days on the road once loaded, with mandatory driver rest built in. Interstate permits and tolls across several states are handled by us, not by you.

Over long distances a dedicated truck for one household is expensive, so most customers choose a part-load. Booking early matters more here, because departures are scheduled.

We also handle the return leg, Ranchi to Bangalore, and onward moves from Ranchi to other towns in Jharkhand. Call +91 98125-18027 with your inventory and the destination pin code for a written quote.

How a Bangalore to Ranchi move runs

  1. Survey. A free pre-move survey at your Bangalore address, or over a video call, to list what is actually going.
  2. Written quote. An itemised price covering packing material, labour, the vehicle, tolls and any optional transit insurance.
  3. Packing day. Our crew packs, labels and prepares an inventory before anything is loaded.
  4. Loading and dispatch. Goods are loaded and the consignment is dispatched towards Ranchi on NH44 to Vijayawada, then NH16 and NH20 inland.
  5. Transit updates. You get a contact number for the vehicle and updates until it reaches Jharkhand.
  6. Delivery and unpacking. Unloading at the Ranchi address, with unpacking and reassembly if that was included in the quote.

What decides the cost from Bangalore to Ranchi

  • Distance. At roughly 1,750 km this is a long-haul run, and fuel, tolls and driver time scale with it.
  • Dedicated vs shared vehicle. A dedicated truck leaves when you want and goes direct. A shared consignment is cheaper but departs when the load is made up.
  • Volume. A one-bedroom consignment and a four-bedroom consignment need very different vehicles.
  • Packing scope. Full professional packing, partial packing, or transport only.
  • Access at both ends. Floor, lift availability and whether a truck can reach the door in Bangalore and in Ranchi.
  • Add-ons. Transit insurance, vehicle transport, storage and unpacking are quoted separately.

We do not quote a per-kilometre rate without seeing the inventory, because it is the volume and the access, not just the distance, that decide the real cost.

Bangalore to Ranchi moving questions

How far is Ranchi from Bangalore by road?

About 1,750 km. Goods travel along NH44 to Vijayawada, then NH16 and NH20 inland, passing Vijayawada, Visakhapatnam and Sambalpur. We plan it as a long-haul intercity route.

How long will my goods take to reach Ranchi?

Once loaded, a goods vehicle on this route is typically 4 to 6 days on the road, plus the packing and loading day at the Bangalore end. Weather, checkpoints and the exact addresses change this, so we confirm a committed window in writing with your quote.

Can you transport my car or bike to Ranchi as well?

Yes. Vehicles travel on a carrier or in an enclosed body and are quoted as a separate line on the same job, so you deal with one team for both.

Is my consignment insured in transit?

Transit insurance is available and is quoted as an optional line item. We will explain what it does and does not cover before you decide.

Do you also move from Ranchi back to Bangalore?

Yes, and onward from Ranchi to other towns in Jharkhand. Tell us both addresses and we will quote the direction you need.
