Common questions about moving in Kannuru

What does a move in Kannuru cost?

There is no fixed rate. The price depends on how much is going, the floor and lift access at both ends, whether you want full packing or transport only, and the destination. We survey first and then give you an itemised written quote.

Do you supply the packing material?

Yes. Cartons, bubble wrap, corrugated sheets, stretch film and tape are included in the packing scope. You do not need to buy anything separately.

Can you store my goods if my new place is not ready?

Yes. Short and long-term storage is available and is quoted separately, so you only pay for the period you actually need.

How much notice do you need?

Two to three days is comfortable for a local move. Month-end and weekends fill up fastest, so earlier is better if your date is fixed.
